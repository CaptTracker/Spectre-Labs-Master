### What was built
- Three new vehicle types (indices 26 to 28, appended: saved vehicles are not affected): `siege_tower` (medium, top platform 6 blocks over the ground), `siege_tower_large` (9 blocks) and `mantlet`. Spawner items `<name>_spawner` with five material levels like the other vehicles,
  research recipes `recipe/research/vehicles/<name>_level_0..4.json` (towers: research `siege_warfare`, level 4 `advanced_siege_warfare`, the wood of the level, mobility units, string, iron and equipment bays; the mantlet is cheap: 3 wood of the level and 2 string, research `siege_warfare`),
  tooltips, item icons, Jade lines, a HUD (pushers and the bridge state), a key for the bridge. Per-vehicle config (enabled, health, speed) comes with the new names automatically.
- **Siege tower.** A 3 x 3 box as tall as the top platform (medium 6, large 9 by default; health 300 / 450, speed 0.05 / 0.04 blocks a tick, material factors as for every vehicle). It is a ground vehicle of its own entity type `siege_tower` (like the ship, it carries its part entities from the
  constructor on). It is not predicted by the client (it follows the server, like the horse carts).
  - **Seats** (number keys follow this order): the driver (steers, stands at the back, pushes too), the ladder seats (2 medium, 3 large), the pushers (2 / 4, behind the driver) and the platform seats (4 / 6). A player who uses the tower boards in the order driver, ladder, pushers, platform; NPC crew take the driver and then the pusher seats.
  - **Push.** Each rider in the driver or a pusher seat adds an equal share of the top speed: 3 pushers (medium) or 5 (large) give the full speed, nobody pushing means it does not move or turn (it also rolls to a stop at once when the pushers leave). Config `siege_push_speed_scale` multiplies the result.
    A pushed vehicle stops dead at a wall (the step height is 0, the speed goes to 0 on a collision) and only has 40% of its turn rate as the least with at least one pusher.
  - **Horses.** The tower is horse-drawn as well (`isHorseDrawn`: up to 2 horses, the hitch API of the carts, a lead used on it): the hitched horses are held to 30% of their walking speed (`getTowSpeedScale`) and the tower is never pulled faster than 1.3 times its top speed. While a horse is hitched the horse moves it
    (the driver steers the lead horse as with a cart) and the pushers add nothing.
  - **Ladder: ladder seats, not walkable parts.** A rider in a ladder seat is lifted along the ladder: his height is a synced list of floats (`lift`, one per seat), moved by the server at `siege_climb_speed` (0.12 blocks a tick) while the rider holds forward (up) or reverse (down),
    sent as the payload `SiegeCommand` (CLIMB_UP, CLIMB_DOWN, CLIMB_STOP, only when the keys change). At the top he is moved to a free platform seat by himself. From a platform seat the number key of a ladder seat takes him to the top of the ladder to climb down. Reason for the choice: walkable parts inside the tower
    (floors with a hole for the ladder that turn and move with the tower) would need the carry of whatever stands on them, which the ship code does only for water hulls, and a rider bound to a seat cannot fall through the floor or be left behind by the tower, whatever its speed.
  - **Drop bridge.** Nine part entities (`BridgePart`, 3 wide and 2 or 3 long by the config `siege_bridge_length`, 0.3 blocks thick, their top at the height of the top platform) in front of the tower. They are walkable (the same kind of box as a ship's deck cell) and solid only while the bridge is fully down.
    The bridge key (default B; payload `SiegeCommand` BRIDGE) lowers it (the tower must stand still) or raises it (nobody may stand on it), for the riders in the driver, the ladder or the platform seats; the lowering takes 24 ticks (the model swings, the planks turn solid at the end). The wish is a synced flag of the vehicle (saved), both sides run the progress at the same pace.
    The tower stands still while its bridge is down or moving, and while somebody who is not aboard stands on its roof (a message tells the driver why). A rider who leaves a platform seat (sneak) while the bridge is down lands on the middle of the bridge and walks over it onto the wall top; with the bridge up, or from the ladder, he lands on the ground behind the tower.
    The roof of the tower (the top of its box) is solid, so a rider who steps out can also stand on it; the server's floating check is reset for players on the roof and the bridge (as on a ship's deck).
  - **Armour.** Arrows (`AbstractArrow`) do 10% of their damage to the front (hit from within 70 degrees of the front: `siege_arrow_damage_front`) and 40% elsewhere (`siege_arrow_damage_other`); fire (burning, flame ammunition) does 2.0 times (`siege_fire_damage_factor`); explosions and the missiles of siege weapons are not changed.
    The armor plates (3 slots) work as for the other vehicles. It can be destroyed (health 0 drops its inventory as ever); a player's blow does normal damage (no break-into-item rule).
  - **NPCs.** NPC crew can take the pusher seats (`SeatRole.PUSHER`; NPC crew may now take the siege roles as well as the weapon seats). A siege engineer (owned or faction) in the driver seat who has a target (an enemy or an attack order block) pushes the vehicle in a straight line to within 2.5 blocks of it (`NpcAISiegeAdvance`);
    engineers take a free weapon before a tower or mantlet (`NpcAIFindVehicle`). With config `siege_auto_bridge` a tower with an NPC aboard that has been pushed against a wall for a second lowers its bridge by itself; NPCs in ladder seats then climb up by themselves, move to the platform and step out onto the bridge.
- **Mantlet.** A 1.3 x 2.6 box with a shield model 3 blocks wide and 2.5 high, one standing rider behind it (the driver seat) who pushes it (speed 0.1, health 60, 3 armor slots, climbs steps of 0.6 only). The pick radius is 0.85, so the whole shield can be aimed at. It stops arrows, bolts and missiles that fly into its front for whatever stands behind it:
  every tick `MantletShield` looks at the projectiles (vanilla `Projectile` and the missiles of the vehicle module) within 10 blocks and stops each one whose last step went through the shield rectangle (3 x 2.5, 0.55 blocks in front of the middle) from its front side to its back side. The projectile is removed, an arrow does its damage to the mantlet
  (the arrow's speed times its base damage; other projectiles 3), a missile has its impact on the mantlet first (so explosive ammunition goes off at the shield and does its damage to it). The box of the mantlet is not hittable by projectiles (`canBeHitByProjectile` is false), so shots from behind, from the sides or over the top pass: archers can shoot past or over it.
  Config `mantlet_blocks_projectiles` and `mantlet_damage_factor`.
- Network: the payload `SiegeCommand(entityId, action byte)` (client to server) is accepted only from a rider of that vehicle (`Authority.PASSENGER`), the climb actions only from a ladder seat, the bridge action only from a driver, ladder or platform seat, any other action byte is ignored, and the bridge has a half second cooldown and its own conditions.
  `VehicleNetwork.VERSION` went from "4" to "5": client and server must match.

### Config (`ancientwarfarevehicle-common.toml`, section `siege`)
`siege_tower_medium_height` (6, 3..12), `siege_tower_large_height` (9, 4..14), `siege_bridge_length` (3, 2..3), `siege_push_speed_scale` (1.0), `siege_climb_speed` (0.12), `siege_arrow_damage_front` (0.1), `siege_arrow_damage_other` (0.4), `siege_fire_damage_factor` (2.0),
`siege_auto_bridge` (true), `mantlet_blocks_projectiles` (true), `mantlet_damage_factor` (1.0). The heights are read when the vehicle types are made, so they need a restart, and they must match on client and server (like the vehicle health: the common config is not synced). The model is built from the same value.

### Deviations and choices
- Ladder seats instead of walkable parts inside the tower (reason above); the top platform is the roof of the box, the bridge is made of walkable part entities.
- The bridge is always lowered at the height of the top platform: a wall top at another height is reached by a short drop or a jump. The model draws a 3 block bridge whatever `siege_bridge_length` says (the walkable part is 2 or 3 blocks).
- Nothing carries what stands on the roof of a tower that is pushed: the tower refuses to move while somebody who is not aboard stands on it. Entities standing on the bridge cannot be carried either: the bridge cannot be raised with someone on it.
- Large tower pushers: 4 seats, but the second row of pushers stands 1 block behind the first, so their hands do not reach the bars in the model by much (the seat and model positions are estimates).
- No new art: the textures are derived from the plank area of the chest cart textures like the carts and ships (an atlas of four zones: plank, dark wood, iron and a woven hide), the item icons are drawn from the same palette.
- A tower that is hitched is pulled by its horses at 30% of their walking speed; a second pair of horses is not supported (the pair slots of the cart code).
- NPCs: no NPC fetches or boards players or soldiers onto the ladder seats yet and there is no pathing for the tower (straight line only); `NpcAISiegeAdvance` is the first piece of the NPC siege AI of the roadmap ("soldiers ordered to attack a walled target use towers" is still open). The seats are not saved across a reload (the existing vehicle rule), the ladder heights are not saved.
- The in-game manual (a book item of the Core module) has no vehicle pages, so nothing was added there; Jade shows the pushers, the bridge and the health.
- Solid roof: the box of the tower is solid (`canBeCollidedWith`), so players cannot walk into the tower and can stand on its top; the walkable inside is only the ladder seats.

## Stage: NPC siege AI

Code, NPC (`mods/ancientwarfarenpc`): new package `ai/siege/{SiegeOps, SiegeJob, SiegeDuty, SiegeRole, SiegeKind, WallInfo, SiegePlanner, NpcAISiegeDuty}`, `entity/NpcCombat` (duty fields, goal, `setPlayerCommand` hook), `ai/owned/NpcAIPlayerOwnedFollowCommand` (steps aside while an NPC has a duty), `npccommand/NpcCommand` (the order and the baton message), `settlement/SettlementConfig` (section `siege`), `en_us.json` (thoughts, census tasks, messages).
No change in the Vehicles module: the stage 1 vehicles already did the ladder climb, the step out onto the bridge and the auto lower of the bridge.

### What was built
- **The order.** No new baton mode: the attack mode aimed at a block already sends `ATTACK_AREA`, and when that block is a wall or a gate (at least `siege_wall_min_height` solid blocks from it up, or an Ancient Warfare gate entity, door or fence gate block within 2 to 2.5 blocks of it) the order is a siege order. Otherwise it is the plain walk to the area order, as before.
  The soldiers (soldier and commander subtypes) and archers of the baton look for towers, mantlets and rams of their owner (`Owner` team rule, not hitched, not a ship) within `siege_search_radius` of themselves and take roles, nearest first. The baton shows an action bar line
  "Siege order: X of Y NPC(s) have siege roles (n tower, n mantlet, n ram crewed)", or says that no equipment fits the wall. Medics, engineers (hammer), NPCs with no weapon and workers take no roles. NPCs of the order who got no role, and everybody once their duty is over, go on with the plain attack area order.
  An NPC with an attack order at a wall and no duty (after a load, or a replacement) asks for one by itself every 10 seconds; a duty that ended at a target is not asked for again at that target for 3 minutes (a new baton order at it resets that).
- **Towers.** Only towers whose top platform is level with the wall top: platform height (ground in front of the wall plus the tower's platform) from one block below to two above the standing level on top of the wall. Up to `siege_max_towers` (2). Melee soldiers fill the pusher seats (3 medium, 5 large: the full speed; archers help only when there is no mantlet to push) and then the climbers (as many as the ladder and platform seats: 6 and 9).
  The first pusher takes the driver seat and steers, the next ones the pusher seats (a pusher takes over the driver seat when it is empty). The job's brain (`SiegeJob`, run by whichever of the crew ticks first) plans a ground route with `SiegePlanner` (A star over 1 x 1 columns, 5 x 5 footprint without corners and the tower's height of free room, drops of 1 block allowed, no climbing, spread over ticks at 300 steps a tick,
  at most `siege_plan_nodes` steps and `siege_plan_range` blocks from the start, a goal that cannot be reached exactly ends at the nearest reached cell if that is within 4 blocks, waypoints cut down by line-of-sight shortcuts) to a point 4.5 blocks out from the target block on the side the tower comes from (the four horizontal directions, the one facing the tower first, with a free column in front of the wall),
  follows it, then pushes straight into the wall until its front is within 0.8 blocks of the wall face (within 2.5 blocks sideways of the target). It then stops and lowers the bridge by itself (`SiegeHelper.toggleBridge`, whatever `siege_auto_bridge` says). No headway for `siege_stuck_ticks` (160) with a driver and a pusher aboard means a new plan from where it stands, twice; then it gives up.
  Climbers walk to the tower, take a ladder seat (waiting for one, up to `siege_dock_wait_ticks`), ride along (inside the tower, so they are covered while it moves), and the tower's own code lifts them up once it is docked, moves them to a platform seat and puts them out onto the bridge; then they walk to the top of the target block and their duty ends, so the normal combat goals fight on the wall. NPCs on the far side follow normal combat.
  Pushers get off when the bridge is down and climb too if the ladder has room. The job is over when no pusher or climber is left, or `siege_dock_wait_ticks` (1800) after the bridge went down. The bridge stays down.
- **Mantlets.** Up to `siege_max_mantlets` (3). One archer per mantlet pushes it (the driver seat), up to `siege_mantlet_cover_archers` (3) more follow it. The mantlet plans its route to a point `siege_mantlet_stop_distance` (28, never more than the archers' attack range minus 4) from the target, along the line from the target to where it starts, and holds there facing the target.
  Covering archers stand in two rows behind the shield (2.3 and 3.3 blocks behind the middle, 0.9 to the side), follow it while it moves and shoot at their attack target (the target goals pick it) within `archer_attack_range` through the normal ranged attack call. If the shield is in the line to the target (a segment test against the 3 x 2.5 shield, the same plane as `MantletShield` uses) or the line is blocked, the archer steps out to the side of the shield for up to 50 ticks, shoots,
  and goes back (about 8 ticks after the shot). The pusher shoots from his seat once the mantlet holds. Cover ends after `siege_cover_max_ticks` (6000).
- **Rams.** Only when the order has a gate (`siege_ram_enabled`); up to `siege_max_rams` (2), one melee soldier each, in the driver seat. The ram plans a route to 6 blocks out from the gate (on the side it comes from), drives at the gate until its head (`getMissileOffset`) is within 1.3 blocks of the gate's box (or it is stopped by it), turns to face it and swings (`handleFireUpdate`) until the gate is open, gone or the job fails.
  A gate that is a door or fence gate block needs the ram's block breaking (`battering_ram_breaks_blocks`, on by default): with it off, or after 60 swings that did nothing, the crew gives up.
- **Faction NPCs: not done.** There are no faction war parties or raids in the mod yet (the roadmap says raids are not in this phase), so there is nothing to attach the AI to. The jobs and the duty are written for `NpcCombat`; a faction version needs a war party that gives the orders and a duty field on `NpcFaction`.
- **Feedback.** Doing keys (look-at tooltips of Jade and WTHIT, census, talk screen "now" lines): `siege.walk`, `siege.push`, `siege.ride`, `siege.climb`, `siege.cross`, `siege.ram`, `siege.cover`, `siege.shoot` (census texts "To siege engine", "Pushing siege engine", "Riding the tower", "Climbing the tower", "Crossing to the wall", "Ramming the gate", "Taking cover", "Shooting from cover").
  Event thoughts: `siege_blocked`, `siege_no_path`, `siege_ram_futile`, `siege_no_seat`, `siege_lost`, `siege_crossed`. When a job fails the owner (if online) gets an action bar line once per job with the reason. The baton line is described above.

### Config (`ancientwarfarenpc-settlements.toml`, section `siege`; `SettlementConfig`)
`siege_ai_enabled` (true), `siege_search_radius` (48), `siege_wall_min_height` (3), `siege_max_towers` (2), `siege_max_mantlets` (3), `siege_max_rams` (2), `siege_mantlet_cover_archers` (3), `siege_mantlet_stop_distance` (28), `siege_plan_range` (128), `siege_plan_nodes` (6000), `siege_stuck_ticks` (160), `siege_ram_enabled` (true), `siege_cover_max_ticks` (6000), `siege_dock_wait_ticks` (1800).

### Deviations and choices
- No separate "siege" baton mode: the attack mode on a block is the siege order when the block is a wall (the closest fit; a new mode would have needed a network change for nothing). A natural cliff or hillside of 3 solid blocks counts as a wall too.
- Jobs are kept in memory only (per level, per piece of equipment) and are not saved: after a reload the saved attack orders give the NPCs new duties by themselves, and the equipment is planned anew (a half-way tower plans from where it stands). Seats are not saved either (the existing vehicle rule), so crew that were riding walk to the vehicle again.
- Only a driver that is an NPC with a duty in the job steers. A tower or mantlet that a player or a siege engineer drives is only pushed by the crew (the engineer's own straight-line advance of stage 1 steers it); a ram or mantlet with such a driver is not crewed. Siege engineers get no duties from the baton order: they keep their stage 1 behaviour.
- The route is planned over open ground only: it does not open doors or gates, climb or bridge anything, and treats leaves and any block with a collision box as walls for the whole height of the tower. Narrow gaps (less than 5 blocks for a tower, 3 for a mantlet) are not used. The planner does not know about other vehicles and entities.
- Tower climbers cross at the middle of the bridge (the place the tower's dismount puts them) and walk to the top of the target block: a wall with a parapet at the target, or a bridge that does not reach the wall top (height outside the -1 to +2 rule), is not handled beyond the height rule.
- A docked tower keeps its bridge down after the job; nothing raises it.
- Archers help push a tower only when no mantlet is in reach; they do not climb.
- A crew member who is attacked while on foot (a foe within 8 blocks) fights first and returns to the duty afterwards; riders and cover archers keep to their duty.

## Final check

- Reviewed the whole uncommitted diff. Confirmed by reading: the drop bridge is nine part entities and never places real blocks (nothing to clean up or grief); the tower has `maxUpStep` 0 and stops dead on `horizontalCollision`; `SiegeCommand` is checked against the sender's own seat (`Authority.PASSENGER`, role per action, unknown actions ignored); `bridgeDown` is only read from NBT with a default, so old saves load; the mantlet only stops projectiles whose last step crossed the front plane inside the 3 x 2.5 rectangle (from behind, beside or above passes); client classes (`ModelSiegeTower`, `ModelMantlet`, input and HUD) are in the client packages; every new vehicle has its models, textures, lang entries and research recipes.
- Fixed: a siege job whose vehicle nobody steers (a player drives it, or the driver left) and a job that drags on had no overall limit, so the pushers could push for ever. `SiegeJob` now fails with `siege_blocked` after `max(600, 4 x siege_stuck_ticks)` ticks of an unsteered advance or dock, or 12000 ticks (10 minutes) in one advance or dock phase. A pusher that never reaches its vehicle gives up after 1200 ticks (`siege_no_path`); its walk timer was being reset every tick and now is not.
- Left as known limits (see the deviations above): the mantlet's collision box is 1.3 blocks wide while its shield is 3, so the shield can pass through a wall edge; any living non-rider on a tower's roof (a mob, too) holds the tower still.
- `./gradlew build` passes. `./gradlew :ancientwarfarereforged:runServer` reached `Done (3.6s)` with no errors from the mods; the only warnings were the expected offline-mode ones and "config is not correct. Correcting" for the vehicle common config and the NPC settlements config (the new `siege` keys were added to the existing files). The server was stopped and the Gradle daemon stopped; no `eula.txt` was created.
- Still not play-tested in a game.
