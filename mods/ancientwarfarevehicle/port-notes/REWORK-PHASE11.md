### Route planner (Vehicles)
- `ShipRoutePlanner` is the breadth first search of `DockRoute` over 1 x 1 water cells (2+ deep, nothing solid above, unloaded chunks count as land) plus the corner cutting, with three additions: a `clearance` (a cell is clear if the square of that many cells round it is deep water: 1 for the fishing boat as before, 2 for the cog),
  `planBetween` for routes of any length up to 400 blocks (the search square holds both ends plus a margin; the deep water test is cached for every column of it), and `partial`: if the goal cannot be reached it returns the route to the reached cell nearest to the goal (`Plan.complete` false).
  `findOpenWater` finds the water cell nearest to a point where a hull of a clearance floats in water of a depth, `waterDepth` and `Plan.length` help the callers. The fishing dock's routes are unchanged (`DockRoute.plan` calls it with clearance 1).
- `ISeaDock` (Core, `extends IBoatWorksite, IOwnable`): `getDockPos()` and `getDockStorage()`. `DockBlockEntity` returns its main inventory (27 slots) as the storage.
- The cog already has a cargo hold (54 slots, `storageBaySize`), so the ship needed no change. Any ship with at least 27 slots of storage can be a cargo ship.

### Sea trade routes for the player
- **The chart.** The item `sea_route` ("Sea Route Chart") holds up to 16 stops in the data component `ancientwarfarenpc:sea_route`: for each stop the dock position, the wait at the dock (seconds in the screen, default 10, at most an hour), an unload list and a load list.
  Docks are added with the chart: sneak and use it on a dock block (a plain use on a dock opens the dock's own screen), or the primary alt key while looking at it (5 blocks). The dock must be of the player's owner (no owner, the same player or the same actor); the message says if not.
  A use anywhere else opens the editor (`OrderMenu`, like the other order slips): per stop up, down, delete, the wait, and the entries. The entries are the trade order's deposit entries (unload: from the ship's hold into the dock) and withdraw entries (load: from the dock into the hold), so the same quantity rules apply
  (unload: all of, a quantity, the excess over a quantity; load: all of, fill up to a quantity). The server accepts only stops the chart already has (no invented docks), at most 16 entries per list.
- **The trader.** The chart goes into the orders slot of an owned trader (`NpcTrader.isValidOrdersStack` now accepts it besides the trade order; with a chart the trader has no shop list). `SeaTrade.tick` (from the trader's tick, every 10 ticks) is a state machine saved on the trader (`sea`: phase, ship, stop, counters):
  - IDLE: every 2 seconds looks for a cargo ship within `sea_trade_bind_range` (32): a ship with 27+ storage slots, of its owner (or of nobody), afloat, whose helm is free or held by an owned NPC of the same owner, and that no other trader has.
  - BOARDING: `NpcAISeaTrade` (priority 3, move flag) walks or swims to the ship with `ShipApproach` and boards the helm, or, if an owned helmsman sits there (the helmsman and trader pair), a passenger or rower seat. Three failed tries or no seat give the ship up (the owner is told) and the trader looks again after 30 seconds.
  - SAILING: the captain is the trader at the helm or the owned NPC helmsman. The trader plans the leg (`ShipRoutePlanner.planBetween`, clearance of the hull, from the ship to the mooring of the next dock) and gives it to the captain with `NpcPlayerOwned.setSailRouteOrder`; `NpcAIShipHelm` (now on `ShipSteering`) passes the waypoints without
    stopping, steers round what the plan did not know (look-ahead) and stops at the mooring. How the order ended is kept (`takeSailResult`: ARRIVED or STUCK).
  - MOORED: the ship is held at the dock (autopilot stop), the stop's unload list is applied against the dock's storage and then its load list, in this order of storages: the dock's inventory first, then (config `sea_trade_settlement_storage`, default on) the linked storages of the settlement the dock lies in (hall inventory first, as the wagon routes do).
    The trader waits the stop's wait time and the ship sails on to the next stop, round again after the last.
- **The mooring** of a dock is the water cell nearest to the dock block (within 70% of `sea_trade_moor_range`, 14 blocks by default) where a hull of the ship's size floats in water 2+ deep (`SeaDocks.mooringOf`), else the dock's own berth. A ship that has arrived there (the helmsman's rule: stopped within the arrival distance) and is within `sea_trade_moor_range` + 12 of the dock counts as moored.
- **Failures.** A route that cannot be planned, a ship that gets stuck (the helmsman's no-clear-way and no-headway rules), or a leg that takes longer than `sea_trade_leg_ticks` is a failed try; three tries give the stop up for this round (the owner is told once, the ship sails on to the next stop, the route is untouched). A dock that is gone or not the owner's, and a leg longer than `sea_trade_max_leg` (256), skip the stop the same way.
  A dock that is **not loaded** cannot trade: the ship sails to the last reachable point (`planBetween` with `partial`: the loaded water cell nearest to the dock), holds there, asks again every 5 seconds, and goes on to the next stop after `sea_trade_dock_wait_ticks` (12000). A **destroyed ship** (gone from a loaded place for 10 seconds, or sinking) is forgotten at once: the owner is told, the trader steps out of it, and the chart,
  the stop and the counters are kept; the trader takes the next cargo ship it finds within range and goes on from the stop it was at. A player at the helm is left alone (the trader waits); an empty helm is taken by the trader.
- Who moves the goods is the trader (instantly, once, when the ship is moored, as the wagon routes load); couriers keep working as before (see deviations).

### Faction trade ships
- **Scheduler.** `CaravanScheduler` rolls trade ships on a clock of their own (`CaravanData.lastSeaRoll`, saved): every `sea_caravan_interval_days` (3) each settlement that has a dock with deep water access (`SeaCaravanSpawner.hasSeaAccess`: a mooring for a 5 x 5 hull in water `sea_caravan_min_depth` (3) deep) has a `sea_caravan_chance` (0.35), if it has fewer than
  `sea_caravan_max_per_settlement` (1) ships and the world fewer than `sea_caravan_max_active` (3). The faction is drawn as for caravans by land (`eligibleFactions`, NEUTRAL or better). A decided ship that finds no place is kept as a pending request (`CaravanData.Pending.sea`) and retried every 5 seconds for 5 minutes. Active ships are `CaravanData.seaActive` (by leader), records older than 3 days are dropped.
- **The ship.** `SeaCaravanSpawner.spawn`: the stock is drawn exactly as for a caravan (`CaravanStock`: two lists by faction and biome, the same trade list), the ship is a cog at material level 0 with no owner, out at sea 48 to 96 blocks (`sea_caravan_min_route` + up to 48) from the mooring, on loaded water where the cog floats, in an entity-ticking chunk, within `caravan_player_range` of a player and at least 48 blocks from every player
  (out of view), with a water route of at least `sea_caravan_min_route` (48) blocks to the mooring. The crew is faction soldiers: a helmsman at the helm, `sea_caravan_rowers` (2) on the benches, `sea_caravan_guards` (2) at the gun then in passenger seats, and the faction trader (a passenger). All are marked as members of the caravan (`caravanLeader`), so the allied-helm rule of faction crews does not make them leave the ship (`NpcFaction.isCaravanMember`).
- **Behaviour** (`SeaCaravanLogic`, run by the leader through the caravan code): the same phases and state as a caravan, `CaravanState.ship` marks it. APPROACH: the leader plans the route and sails with the shared steering (`ShipSteering`) with the helmsman's ship; arrived at the mooring it becomes TRADING for `sea_caravan_stay_ticks` (9000) and the players who manage the settlement within 200 blocks get a chat line. TRADING: the ship is held at the dock; players come alongside or aboard and use the normal faction trade view of the trader (reputation based prices, hostile refused, the trade reputation and its daily cap are the same code);
  every 3 seconds the trader fills the buy orders of the settlement's market stalls within `sea_caravan_order_range` (64) of it through the same `CaravanLogic.fillBuyOrders` (goods are made only for the one paid deal, the coins go into the ship's hold, the rest of the goods is dropped from existence). LEAVING: the ship sails back to where it came from and the whole ship (crew, trader, hold) vanishes once no player is within `caravan_despawn_range` (64) of the trader, or after 12000 ticks.
  A route that cannot be planned three times, or a ship that gets stuck three times, on the way in turns the ship back; a ship with no helmsman alive does not sail (it lies where it is until the caravan's time runs out).
- **Killed.** The same rules as a caravan: killing the trader drops the coins the ship earned (the hold holds nothing else), frees the crew (they stop escorting and may despawn) and leaves the ship where it is, empty; reputation for the kill is the existing faction kill rule. A ship that sinks (the vehicle's own rule, 200 ticks) drops its hold's coins when it breaks up, and the crew and trader are freed (`abandon`).
- `/awcaravan sea [faction]` (op) sends a trade ship at once to the settlement at or nearest to you.

### Deviations and choices
- Per-stop rules live on the chart (the "route item"), not on the dock: the same dock can be a stop of several routes with different rules. The dock screen was not changed.
- The trader is the helmsman (or sits beside an owned helmsman); there is no separate "courier" step: the trader moves the goods itself, instantly, at the moment the ship is moored (as the wagon routes do). Couriers still treat the dock's inventory as an output (existing dock behaviour), so goods meant for loading should come from the settlement's linked storages (on by default), or be put in a dock that is outside any settlement.
- Entities only act in loaded chunks, so a route only runs while its ships are near a player. A ship in an unloaded chunk waits there; the "dock not loaded" case is the one that is handled in the ship's own loaded area. Routes are limited to 400 blocks between two docks (config `sea_trade_max_leg` 256 by default).
- The faction trade ship has its own scheduler clock and pending list inside the caravan classes instead of a copy of the scheduler; the steering of the helmsman was moved into `ShipSteering` so the owned helmsmen and the faction helmsman share it (behaviour of the owned helmsman unchanged). The faction helmsman's steering is run by the leader's tick, not by an AI goal of its own.
- A moored ship lies up to about 14 + 6 blocks from the dock block (the nearest water where a cog fits), so a player has to swim to the ship and climb the gangway rows to trade with the trader on deck (the interaction range is a few blocks). The trade view itself is unchanged.
- The model of the chart is the trade order's texture (no new art).
- Seats are not saved across a reload (existing vehicle rule): after a reload the riders get seats in their saved order; the helmsman is first, so it keeps the helm in the normal case.

## Stage: naval combat (ramming, grappling hooks, boarding, sinking cargo, helmsman)

Code, Vehicles: `entity/ship/{GrappleHook, FloatingCrate, ShipHelper (ram, pull, lines, damage memory), ShipSpec}`, `entity/types/VehicleTypeCog`, `entity/VehicleBase` (stance, `ramHurt`), `item/GrapplingHookItem`, `init/AwVehicles` (entity types `grapple_hook`, `floating_crate`, item `grappling_hook`),
`client/render/{GrappleHookRenderer, FloatingCrateRenderer}`, `client/VehicleRenderClient`, `client/screen/GuiVehicleInventory` (stance toggle), `network/{VehicleEntityPayloads.StanceCommand, VehicleNetwork}` (`VERSION` is now "4"), `entity/VehicleSettings`, `config/AWVehicleStatics`.
NPC: `ai/vehicle/{ShipCombat, NpcAIShipBoard, NpcAIShipCrew, NpcAIShipHelm}`, `event/ShipPrizeEvents`, `entity/{NpcCombat, faction/NpcFaction}` (goal), `caravan/SeaCaravanSpawner` (faction mark), `init/AwNpcs`, `settlement/SettlementConfig`.
Assets: item model `grappling_hook` (vanilla lead texture), research recipe `recipe/research/misc/grappling_hook.json` (research `naval_warfare`: 3 iron ingots, 4 strings), lang keys in both `en_us.json` files (item, tooltip, entity names, messages, stance button, thoughts `ship.ram` and `ship.flee`, census tasks).

### Ramming
- Ship types carry the ram in `ShipSpec` (`ram(damage, reach)`, `hasRam()`): war galley 35 (strong), cog 12 (weak, new, tooltip added), fishing boat none. The old galley-only ram was reworked in `ShipHelper.ram()`:
  the bow box hits the first thing it touches (a ship's hull part counts as the ship, once per target). The damage is `base * ship_ram_damage_scale * min(1.5, closing speed / top speed)`, where the closing speed is the rammer's speed along its heading less the target's speed along it; below `ship_ram_min_speed` nothing happens.
- A struck ship, vehicle or boat takes it on the hull (`VehicleBase.ramHurt`: not blocked by the 10 tick invulnerability of blows; a ship goes down by it as by any damage). The rammer takes `ship_ram_recoil_fraction` of it (only against ships, boats and vehicles) and loses speed (50%, 20% against living things).
  Living things and items on the struck deck are knocked along the heading (`ship_ram_deck_knock`), with wood splinters and splash particles and the wood hit sounds. Cooldown `ship_ram_cooldown_ticks` (30). Ships of the same owner, and ships grappled to the rammer, are not rammed; hooks and crates are ignored.

### Grappling hooks
- Item `grappling_hook` (durability 64, one point a throw). Use while on a ship (deck or seat) aiming at another ship within `grapple_range` (24 blocks hull to hull; aim cone of 13 degrees on any hull cell) throws a hook. Sneak and use lets go of the lines the player holds (the ship's owner: all lines of the ship).
- `GrappleHook` is one entity per line, not saved, sitting on the target hull at the cell nearest the source (the rope is drawn from the anchor on the source ship, a strip like the hitch rope, slack while flying, tight once held; the hook item spins at the end).
  FLYING 6 ticks, PULLING (`grapple_pull_speed` 0.08 blocks a tick, split by weight so the lighter ship moves more, both ships also turn side by side, parallel or anti-parallel, 0.96 degrees a tick at most) until the nearest hull cells touch and the headings are within 15 degrees, then HELD.
  The pull goes through `ShipHelper.addPull`, applied inside `ShipHelper.step` with the same shallow water and block tests as the ship's own movement, so a ship cannot be pulled aground (it just stops). While HELD: slack costs nothing; beyond the rest length (distance at contact + 1.5) a spring pulls both ways and drags the ship that sails away (its speed is cut by up to 8% a tick); overlapping hulls are pushed apart.
- Lines end when: a blow hits the hook (any damage), shears, a sword or an axe are used on it, the holder (the thrower, player or NPC) is dead or gone or more than 96 blocks away, `grapple_timeout_ticks` (6000, 0 = never) after contact, either ship sinks or goes, or the hooks are `grapple_snap_distance` (40) apart. Limit `grapple_max_lines` (2) a ship. `ShipHelper.alongside()` lists the ships a ship is joined to side by side.
- Crossing for players is walking and jumping over the rails (the decks are side by side, the line stops the ships at contact); the gangway rows and the open bow are the easy places.
- NPC: soldiers in a seat of a ship throw hooks (`ShipCombat.tickCrew`, from `NpcAIShipCrew`) at a hostile ship within `naval_npc_grapple_range` (14), checking every second, one throw per `naval_npc_grapple_cooldown_ticks` (600) for the whole ship. An owned crew only does so when the ship's stance is aggressive; soldiers of an NPC faction only on a ship nobody owns (`naval_faction_boarding`). A ship is hostile when its owner's actor (or, ownerless, the actor of whoever is aboard) is HOSTILE to the NPC's actor, or a player or NPC aboard is hostile to the NPC.
  NPCs do not use up a hook item.

### Boarding
- `NpcAIShipBoard` (priority 1 on owned soldiers and on faction NPCs, holds move and look, not interruptible). `ShipCombat.mission` (recomputed every 10 ticks): a soldier with a melee weapon that sits in a ROWER or PASSENGER seat (or stands on the deck) of a ship that is HELD alongside a hostile ship with a foe aboard boards it; boarders standing on the deck of his own ship make him defend it.
  Gunners, helmsmen and archers stay at their posts (`NpcAIShipCrew` lets go of a soldier with a mission). The soldier leaves his seat, walks (move control; the pathfinder cannot use decks) to the other hull, and within 2.2 blocks of it (or after 4 s) leaps to the nearest deck cell of the other ship (a teleport with a puff of cloud, see deviations), then fights the nearest foe aboard (riders in seats too) with `swing` and `doHurtTarget` every 14 ticks.
  With nobody left to fight (2 s) he hops back, and sits down again on his own ship (a free non-gun seat). Cooldown 100 ticks, at most 90 s a try. `naval_boarding_enabled` turns all of it off.
- Captured: a ship of an NPC faction (the sea trade ships; `SeaCaravanSpawner` marks them with the faction's name) with nobody of an NPC faction alive aboard (riding or on the deck) is a prize: the first player to use it (`ShipPrizeEvents`) becomes its owner and the reputation with the faction drops by Spectre Factions' attack penalty (`adjustReputation(..., "attack")`). `naval_prize_enabled`. A player's ship that is left with no crew needs nothing: its owner's rules (`Owner`) are those of any ship.

### Sinking cargo
- When a ship has sunk (`ShipHelper.sink` ends, before `kill`), `FloatingCrate.releaseCargo`: for each stack of its cargo hold the fraction `sinking_cargo_fraction` (0.5; the count is rounded at random, so a single item floats with that chance) is taken, the rest of the hold is cleared (lost), and the stacks are shared among at most `sinking_crate_max` (8) crates of about 27 stacks that appear in the wreck. Ammo, armor and upgrades still drop as before.
  `FloatingCrate` (a barrel model, no new art) rises to the surface and bobs for `sinking_crate_ticks` (6000), then is lost with its items. A player touching it, or a ship or boat whose hull comes against it, collects it (a ship's hold first, what does not fit and what a boat or a full inventory cannot take drops); a blow breaks it open and the items drop. Nothing can collect it for 2 seconds.
- Faction cargo: the trade ship's hold only ever holds the coins it earned (goods for buy orders are made at the moment of a paid deal, `virtualCargo`), so the same fraction applies and no unpaid goods can float.

### Helmsman
- Stance: `VehicleBase.isAggressive` (synced, saved in the ship's data), the toggle button "Stance: passive / aggressive" in the ship's inventory screen next to Stats and Pack (payload `StanceCommand`, the owner or anyone for an ownerless ship). A baton toggle was not built.
- `ShipCombat.helmTick`, called first in `NpcAIShipHelm.tick`: an aggressive stance and a ship with a ram: the owned helmsman steers at the nearest hostile ship within `naval_helm_ram_range` (48), leading its movement, with full sail and no stop (`setPilot(aim, 1, false)`); when the water ahead is not clear (`isWaterClear`) it gives the normal steering back. Not while grappled.
  Not aggressive: if the ship was hurt in the last 5 seconds (`ShipHelper.ticksSinceHurt`) and `naval_helm_flee_home` is on, it sails for the mooring of the nearest dock of its owner within 256 blocks (loaded docks, `SeaDocks.mayUse`); no dock: it keeps its course. The sail order and the sea route stay and go on afterwards.

### Deviations and choices
- The hook is thrown at a ship found by aim (a cone on the hull cells), not by a ray against the hull; the hook's flight is 6 ticks along a straight line. Lines can only be cut at the hook (a blow or a tool used on it), not along the rope; they do not survive a reload or an unloaded ship (a line is dropped when either ship is not loaded).
- NPC soldiers leap by teleport (with a puff), not by a physical jump: the move control cannot jump over ship rails (they are entities, not blocks). Crossing of players is physical.
- Prize: only for faction ships (the trade ships); "the boarding side's owner may take the helm" is the ordinary use of the ship once it is the player's. Owned soldiers do not crew a prize by themselves; they go back to their own ship.
- Gunners stay on their guns while boarders are on deck (they are not defenders); archers do not board.
- The stance toggle is in the ship's screen only (no baton mode). An NPC's throw does not use a hook item. A hook has the vanilla lead as its icon (no new art), the crate is a vanilla barrel.
- Ram damage uses the existing battering damage type. Ramming is by the bow box only; side collisions do nothing.
- `VehicleNetwork.VERSION` went from "3" to "4" (new payload): client and server must match.

## Final check

Reviewed the whole uncommitted diff of phase 11 (Vehicles, NPCs, Core and Automation) for item duplication, tunnelling and launches from grapple pulls, loops between linked ships, left-behind entities, server authority, client classes on the server, old saves and per-tick costs.

Checked and found sound:
- **Duplication.** The sinking ship's hold is cleared slot by slot before `kill`, so the ship's own drop of its storage is empty and the crates hold the only copy (`FloatingCrate.releaseCargo`). A crate empties its contents before it is removed, and `collect` and `hurt` both stop at `isRemoved`, so a blow and a touch in the same tick give the items once. Dock loading and unloading goes through the item handler API (extract, then insert), and the settlement storages are de-duplicated by handler, so the dock and a linked storage cannot be counted twice. A faction trade ship's hold starts empty and gets only earned coins (`virtualCargo`); killing the trader empties it into coins, sinking floats at most the fraction of those coins.
- **Ships.** Pulls go through `ShipHelper.step` with the same shallow and block tests as the ship's own movement, at most 0.08 blocks a tick, and are cleared at the end of every step (and while sinking), so a pull cannot build up, tunnel or lift a ship. The line entity is `noSave`: it is not saved, and it lets go when either ship is sinking, removed or not loaded, so an unloaded ship cannot leave a half-running pair. The ram has a cooldown (30 ticks), skips linked and same-owner ships and needs a closing speed, so it does not hit every tick.
- **Authority.** The hook target is chosen on the server from the player's look; the client sends nothing. The only new client packet, `StanceCommand`, is checked for reach and ownership. The chart editor accepts only stops the stored chart has. Client classes (screens, renderers) are in `client` packages and are registered from client-only code.
- **Old saves.** Ship data without `aggressive`, caravan data without `ship`, `seaActive`, `lastSeaRoll` or `sea` on pending entries, and a trader without `sea` all load with defaults. Hooks and the sea steering are not saved by design.

Fixed in this check:
- A trade ship whose trader was killed stayed in the sea for ever (it is meant to be a prize, but an empty ship of nobody piles up). `CaravanLogic.onLeaderKilled` now marks it (`ShipPrizeEvents.markAbandoned`), and after one day, with nobody of a faction aboard and no player within `caravan_despawn_range`, it vanishes (`ShipPrizeEvents.onVehicleTick`, checked every 10 seconds on tagged ships only).
