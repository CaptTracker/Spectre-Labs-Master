# Rework phase 10, part 1: family foundations

Not play-tested. `./gradlew build` passes. This part is names, the family registry, couples, homes, morale and the API. Children (births, growing up, school, play) are a separate part and use the API below.

## What changed
- **Surnames.** Every player-owned NPC has a surname (`NpcBase.getSurname/setSurname`, saved as `surname` in the entity data and in the spawner item data, so it survives repacking and resurrection). Existing NPCs roll one at their first tick (`ensureSurname`, right after the first name). The pools are a new `"surnames": [...]` list in each `npc_names` file (default 72, desert 28, eastern 25, nahua 24, norse 26, outlaw 23, roman_greek 28, savage 23). An owned NPC uses the pool of the culture its first name belongs to (a name that is in a culture file, such as Hrolf or Selim, takes that culture's surnames), else the general pool; a faction NPC with a pool of its own would use it (`NpcNames.pickSurname(random, faction, givenName)`). Faction NPCs get no surname.
- **Where the full name shows.** The nameplate keeps its style ("Apprentice Miner Bill"). The full name "Bill Carter" (`NpcBase.getFullName()`, lang key `npcname.full`, so the order can change per language) is the title of the talk screen, the name column of the census and the first line of the Jade and WTHIT tooltip ("Name: Bill Carter"). A custom name (name tag) wins everywhere.
- **Family registry** (`settlement/family/SettlementFamilies`, held by `Settlement.getFamilies()`, saved in the settlement as `families`; a save without it loads as no families):
  - households (`Household`: one or two adults, the children that are not grown up, the claimed home beds and the build project id of the home, the surname, the last birth);
  - the parents of every child (kept after the parents die or the child grows up), the children that are not grown up (`minors`), grief, widow(er) state, shared social time of pairs, and tombstones of dead members.
  - Works on UUIDs: unloaded NPCs are fine. The member record (`Settlement.Member`) got a `surname`.
  - Clean-up: `Settlement.removeMember` (left the settlement, dismissed in the hall, repacked, changed dimension) calls `onMemberLeft` (out of every record, no grief; the partner is on its own, the children stay with the other parent; a household without adults is dissolved). `Settlement.handleNpcDeath` calls `onMemberDied` (a tombstone; the survivor grieves and is a widow(er); the parents of a dead child grieve; the household goes on). Every second a sweep drops what refers to members that are gone (a child that never joined within 5 minutes too).
  - Resurrection: the item form keeps `formerId` (the UUID of the NPC). When the resurrected NPC (new UUID) joins the settlement, `onMemberJoined` finds the tombstone and replaces the old UUID in the records: it goes back to its partner if the survivor is still on its own (their grief ends), a child goes back to its household. If the survivor has moved on, the revived NPC keeps the children that are with nobody as a new household. Tombstones go when the death list is cleared (and at most 100 are kept).
- **Couples** (`FamilyLife`): every second, two members that may become partners (adult, no partner, not a relative, not mourning), both idling at a gathering point (`markSocial` within the last 2 seconds) within `couple_social_radius` blocks add one second of shared time; a sociable one adds `couple_sociable_bonus`, a loner multiplies with `couple_loner_factor`. Once a day (`couple_chance_per_day`) a pair with `couple_bond_seconds` of shared time, both loaded with morale of at least `couple_min_morale`, becomes a couple (at most one new couple per settlement per day, only from `couple_min_population` members). Any two adult members can pair; there is no gender rule. The thought `partnered` ("Bill and Mara are now partners.") goes to both, the founder gets a chat message if online, and hearts show. The household takes the surname of the first partner; the other takes it too (`partners_share_surname`). A widow(er) with children brings the household along; two households merge.
- **Homes** (`FamilyHomes`): a couple claims two beds. First choice is a finished phase 9 house (design category house with the `housing_capacity` tag) where nobody has to move out; then a tent; then two loose beds within 3 blocks of each other; last, a home where sleepers must move out, but only when they find another free bed (a bed swap). A household claims beds, they are not given to other members (`SettlementLife.assignBeds` skips them) and the members sleep in them. The home is claimed before free beds go to homeless members. A bed that is broken ends the home. A household in a tent or loose beds moves into a free house once a day. The beds of a survivor on its own go back to the pool. Beds for children: one more bed of the same building (or a free loose bed within 6 blocks) is claimed when a child is registered.
- **Home for a family in the planner.** `BuildingPlanner.tryHousing` also runs when no member lacks a bed but a couple (or parent with children) has no home (`SettlementFamilies.homesWanted()`, minus the builds already planned for it): it plans a house (a tent only when no house can be afforded) for the reason `family_home` ("because a couple needs a home of its own" on the Building tab).
- **Morale** (`FamilyMorale`, added to `NpcMorale.compute`, named factors in the morale tab): `family_partner` (+3), `family_partner_near` (+2 while the partner is within 10 blocks), `family_home` (+3 when sleeping in a bed of the home), `family_children` (+2 for a parent while a child that is not grown up is within 12 blocks; the hook for the children), `family_grief` (-25, full for the first half of the grief, then fading; 3 days).
- **Talk screen and tooltip.** A "Family" block on the talk screen (partner, children, parents, home or no home, grief). Tooltip lines "Partner: ..." and "Children: n".
- **Thoughts** (3 phrasings each): `partnered`, `found_home`, `partner_died`, `child_died`, `partner_back`.
- **Schedule.** `NpcAIScheduleGoal` picks the same gathering point for both partners of a couple.

## API for the children part (`settlement/family/FamilyApi`, server side)
- `getHousehold(npc | settlement, uuid)`: `Optional<Household>` (`getAdults()`, `getChildren()` = the children that are not grown up, `getHomeBeds()`, `getHomeBuilding()`, `getSurname()`, `getLastBirth()`, `isCouple()`, `hasHome()`).
- `getPartner(...)`: `Optional<UUID>`. `getChildren(...)`: every registered child (grown up or not). `getParents(...)`: the parents of a child. `isMinor(settlement, uuid)`.
- `registerChild(parentA, parentB, childUuid)` (two loaded parents) or `registerChild(settlement, parentA, parentB, childUuid, gameTime)`: puts the child in the household (the parents must be adults of one household; `parentB` may be null), records the parents and the birth time. The child NPC joins the settlement as usual. The registry makes the child take the household surname (`Settlement.syncSurname` forces it, so the children code may leave the surname empty), and gives it a home bed when there is one. False if the parents are not a household.
- `markGrownUp(settlement, childUuid)`: the child leaves the children of its household and is an adult (it may become a partner; the parents stay on record).
- `homeOf(settlement, household)`: `Optional<Home>` (building id or null, beds, centre).
- `canHaveChild(level, settlement, household)` / `canHaveChild(npc)` and `childBlocker(...)` (the reason key): a couple with a home; neither grieving; both of morale at least `child_min_morale`; fewer than `max_children_per_household` children; `birth_cooldown_days` since the last birth; a free bed in the home or room for one more; food in storage for `child_min_food_days` days per member.
- Also: `surnameFor(settlement, household)`, `childTraits(level, settlement, parentA, parentB, random)` (wraps `NpcTraits.inherit` with the traits of loaded parents, or of the member record for unloaded ones), `adulthoodTicks()` (`years_to_adulthood` years of `year_length_days` days), `homesWanted(settlement)`, `foodDaysPerMember(level, settlement)`, `describe(...)` for the screens.

## Files
- New (`settlement/family/`): `SettlementFamilies`, `Household`, `FamilyLife`, `FamilyHomes`, `FamilyMorale`, `FamilyApi`.
- Edited: `entity/NpcNames` (surname pools, `full`), `entity/NpcBase` (surname, `getFullName`), `entity/NpcPlayerOwned` (surname on, `formerId`, surname sync), `settlement/Settlement` (registry, hooks, member surname, `syncSurname`), `settlement/SettlementData` (calls `FamilyLife.tick`), `settlement/SettlementLife` (homes before bed assignment, claimed beds skipped), `settlement/NpcMorale`, `settlement/Census` (surname in the entry and the packet), `settlement/SettlementConfig`, `settlement/building/BuildingPlanner`, `ai/owned/NpcAIScheduleGoal`, `menu/NpcTalkMenu`, `client/screen/GuiNpcTalk`, `client/screen/CensusView`, `compat/info/NpcTooltipInfo`, `lang/en_us.json`, the eight `npc_names/*.json` files.

## Config keys (`ancientwarfarenpc-settlements.toml`, section `families`)
`families_enabled` (true), `year_length_days` (96), `years_to_adulthood` (2.0), `couple_min_population` (4), `couple_min_morale` (60), `couple_bond_seconds` (240), `couple_chance_per_day` (0.3), `couple_social_radius` (8), `couple_sociable_bonus` (0.5), `couple_loner_factor` (0.5), `partners_share_surname` (true), `widow_repartner_days` (6), `grief_days` (3), `grief_morale` (25), `partner_morale` (3), `partner_nearby_morale` (2), `partner_nearby_radius` (10), `family_home_morale` (3), `children_near_morale` (2), `children_near_radius` (12), `child_min_morale` (55), `child_min_food_days` (3.0), `max_children_per_household` (3), `birth_cooldown_days` (8).
`year_length_days` and `years_to_adulthood` are the owner's decision (192 days to adulthood by default); `SettlementConfig.adulthoodTicks()` gives the ticks. The children part must not define them a second time.

## Deviations
- Homes and couples only work while the daily schedule is on (`schedule_enabled`): the bed upkeep that claims homes lives in `SettlementLife.tick`, which does nothing without it.
- A home for a family is "two beds in one finished housing building, or two loose beds near each other". A farmhouse, inn or other non-housing building is never a home. Buildings that the player built by hand (no phase 9 project) count only as loose beds.
- There is no separate "need" entry in the Needs tab for the family home: the hook is the housing reason of the planner (`family_home`) as asked.
- An NPC that changes dimension or is repacked leaves the settlement, so it leaves its family records too (no grief, the partner is on its own).
- The grief and widow state is not shown on the hall screen; only the talk screen (Family block) and the morale tab show it.
- The town hall member list does not show surnames yet (census, talk screen and tooltip do).
- Survivors only get the grief thought when loaded; the morale penalty is kept for unloaded ones.
- Not play-tested: couple forming, home claiming, bed swaps, resurrection, the planner reason and the screens were only checked by compiling and reading.

# Rework phase 10, part 2: children

Not play-tested. `./gradlew build` passes. Births, the child NPC, growing up, population and food, and the admin tools. Builds on part 1 (the family registry, homes and `FamilyApi`).

## What changed
- **A child is the worker entity in a child state, not a new entity type.** Reason: a child must keep its UUID (the family records, bed, member record and tombstones use it) and its name, traits and skills when it grows up; a separate `aw_npc_child` type would mean replacing the entity at the moment of adulthood. `NpcWorker` has a synced `child` flag (`isChild()`, also a hook in `NpcBase` that is false for every other NPC), the age in ticks (`childAge`, saved; the item form too, so a resurrected child keeps its age and does not age as an item) and `childAgeAt` (game time of the last count, so the time its chunk was unloaded counts). The goals depend on the state: `registerGoals` gives a child the child goals and a grown worker the work goals; `rebuildGoals` swaps them at birth, at loading and at coming of age (the ship crew goals of the base class stay).
- **Births** (`FamilyChildren`): once a day (`FamilyLife.tick`, after the couples) every household that passes `FamilyApi.canHaveChild` (couple with a home, morale, no grief, food, cooldown, a bed for one more) has a child with the chance `birth_chance_per_day`. The child appears at the home (or next to a parent when the home is not loaded; at least one parent must be loaded). It gets a first name from the culture pool of its parents (`NpcNames.pickGivenLike`: the culture file that holds the first name of a parent, else the general pool; names in the household are avoided), the surname of the household, traits from `FamilyApi.childTraits` (`NpcTraits.inherit`) and `child_skill_head_start_xp` experience in one of the best skills of the loaded parents. Both loaded parents get the thought `child_born` ("Our child Tom was born", neutral wording), the owner gets a message, and the census, the talk screen and Jade list the child. The child is registered with `FamilyApi.registerChild` (household, parents, birth time), joins the settlement and gets a home bed from the part 1 code.
- **What a child is and does.** Scale: the 1.21 `Attributes.SCALE`, `child_scale_start` (0.5) at birth growing to 1.0 with the age (updated once a second; the hit box follows). No orders (batons do nothing, no work orders, the inventory screen opens the talk screen instead), no role (`setRole` ignores a child, the hall shows "Child" instead of the Role button), no work, no fight. Goals: flee from hostiles (a brave child still runs) and shelter during alarms, eat from the storage (like adults), the daily schedule (sleep in the bed of the home, the evening at the gathering point, rain shelter) and `NpcAIChildPlay` by day: it runs around near the home of its household (else a gathering point or the hall) within `child_play_radius`, follows another child or follows a parent; time with a child or parent counts as social time. The talk screen says what it does ("I'm following Mara around").
- **Food.** `child_hunger_factor` (0.5): a child holds half the food, it runs out half as fast, and in the food of the settlement (food days per member, the food need, the food morale) a child counts as 0.5 member (`Settlement.foodMembers`). A newborn starts with a full food meter.
- **Morale of a child** (simpler factors, `NpcMorale`): hunger, bed, sleep, injury, play (social), fear, friends nearby, `family_home` and the new `family_parent_near` (+`children_near_morale` while a parent is within `children_near_radius`). No inn, tool, idle, skill or trait factors.
- **Growing up.** After `years_to_adulthood * year_length_days * 24000` ticks (192 days by default) `NpcWorker.growUp` makes it a normal worker without a role (name, traits, skills and bed stay; the bed leaves the claim of the household when the home has more than two beds), `FamilyApi.markGrownUp`, a thought for it and for its parents, a message to the owner. It can partner from then on. The age is shown as years and days of the game year ("Tom Carter, child, 1 year 40 days"): the talk screen title and a "Growing up" line in the Family block, the census (the role column says "Child 1y 40d", the name tooltip has the full text), Jade and WTHIT. The numbers are made on the server (`FamilyAge`), the client has no copy of the config.
- **Population.** Children are members: they count in the population, need a bed (the planner's housing reason sees the deficit; a family home asks for room for them), and losing one starts the family grief of part 1 (unchanged). Dismissing or killing a child is treated like an adult; the faction rules are the normal ones.
- **Admin.** Config `family_debug_fast_growth` (multiplier on the age counting) and the command `/awsettlement` (ops, level 2): `birth [parent]` (forces a birth, skipping the morale, food, cooldown and bed checks; needs a household with a loaded adult), `age <child> <days>`, `growup <child>`, `families` (households and children with age).

## Files
- New: `settlement/family/FamilyChildren`, `settlement/family/FamilyAge`, `ai/owned/NpcAIChildPlay`, `command/CommandSettlement`.
- Edited: `entity/NpcWorker` (child state), `entity/NpcBase` (`isChild`, `getChildAgeTicks`, the child nameplate), `entity/NpcNames` (`pickGivenLike`, `child`), `entity/NpcThinking` (no job line for a child), `ai/NpcAIFleeHostiles`, `trait/TraitEffects` (hunger factor), `settlement/Settlement` (member record: `child`, `age`, `ageAt`; `foodMembers`, `childCount`, `childAgeOf`), `settlement/NpcMorale`, `settlement/SettlementMorale`, `settlement/SettlementNeeds`, `settlement/Census` (child age in the entry and the packet), `settlement/SettlementConfig`, `settlement/family/FamilyApi` (`describe`, food days), `settlement/family/FamilyLife` (daily births), `settlement/family/FamilyMorale`, `settlement/family/SettlementFamilies` (bed of a grown child), `menu/NpcTalkMenu`, `menu/TownHallMenu`, `client/screen/CensusView`, `client/screen/GuiTownHallInventory`, `compat/info/NpcTooltipInfo`, `init/AwNpcs` (command), `lang/en_us.json`.

## Config keys (`ancientwarfarenpc-settlements.toml`, section `families`)
`birth_chance_per_day` (0.25), `child_hunger_factor` (0.5), `child_skill_head_start_xp` (40), `child_scale_start` (0.5), `child_play_radius` (12), `family_debug_fast_growth` (1.0). Used from part 1: `year_length_days`, `years_to_adulthood`, `max_children_per_household`, `birth_cooldown_days`, `child_min_morale`, `child_min_food_days`, `children_near_morale`, `children_near_radius`.

## Deviations
- No separate entity type (`aw_npc_child`), see the first point: a child state on the worker keeps the UUID and the whole record when it grows up.
- A child ages with the age counter of the entity: while its chunk is unloaded the age catches up at the next load (by game time, with the debug multiplier). The age shown for an unloaded child in the census is its last known age plus the time since (without the debug multiplier).
- A birth needs at least one loaded parent. A household whose parents are both unloaded has no child that day (the chance comes again the next day).
- The registry (`minors`) and the entity state are two records of the same fact. A child that is dismissed from the settlement is no longer a registered child; it stays a child entity (small, plays) until it grows up, and counts as a member with a child's food weight if it joins a settlement again.
- No school (the roadmap says "if there is one"; no school building exists).
- A child never crews a ship; the brave-fight goal is not part of its goals.
- The hall member list shows "Child" instead of the Role button, but not the age (the census does).
- Not play-tested: births, the goals and the play behaviour, the scale and hit box, growing up, the commands and the screens. Only compiled and read.

# Rework phase 10: final check

Reviewed the whole uncommitted diff (families and children) for runaway population, stale UUIDs, roles and orders for children, double counting, the scale attribute, client classes on the server, old saves, per-tick cost and the permission level of the command.

## Checked and found sound
- **Population:** a birth needs a couple with a home, both parents of morale `child_min_morale`, no grief, fewer than `max_children_per_household` children, `birth_cooldown_days` since the last birth (the birth time is saved in the household, so a reload does not reset it), a free bed in or next to the home, and `child_min_food_days` of food per member (children weigh `child_hunger_factor`). The daily check runs once per game day (`lastDay` is saved). Only the operator command skips the checks.
- **Stale UUIDs:** death keeps a tombstone and cleans partner, parent and child records (the survivor grieves); leaving or dismissal cleans them without grief; a resurrected NPC takes the old place by `formerId`; the registry sweeps ids of members that are gone once a second.
- **Children have no role or orders:** `setRole` refuses a role, `roleFromTool` and the job board skip them, batons, orders stacks and the inventory screen are refused or open the talk screen, and a child has none of the work, fight or horse goals.
- **Counted once:** a child is one member record; only the food weight differs (`foodMembers`).
- **Scale:** `growUp` sets the scale attribute back to 1.0 and swaps the goals back.
- **Server and client:** no client class is used by the family, command, entity, menu or AI code; `runServer` reached `Done (`.
- **Old saves:** every new NBT key (`surname`, `child`, `childAge`, `families`, household, grief and tomb tags) reads as a default when missing.
- **Cost:** the family upkeep and the child age counter run once a second, the births once a day, the surname sync every few seconds, the child play goal every 10 ticks; the town hall and census only read.
- **Command:** `/awsettlement` requires permission level 2.

## Fixed in the final check
- `Settlement.java` had been rewritten with LF line endings (the file is CRLF); it is CRLF again.
- `TraitEffects.workSpeed` gave a child the hunger factor as its base work speed (a copy of the hunger change); it is 1.0 again (a child has no work anyway).
- `FamilyChildren.dailyBirths` rolls the birth chance first and checks the food and the beds only for the households that roll a birth.

`./gradlew build` passes; `:ancientwarfarereforged:runServer` reached `Done (` with no errors from the mods; `eula.txt` was not created. Not play-tested.
