# Ancient Warfare Reforged 1.3.0 (in progress)

Changes since 1.2.0.

**Optional mod support**
- **Dynamic Trees:** with Dynamic Trees installed, the lumberjacks of a tree farm cut its trees down properly: the whole tree falls (with the Dynamic Trees falling animation), the axe wears, and the logs and sticks that drop are collected into the farm. The farm plants Dynamic Trees seeds, bone meal works on its saplings, and one seed of the felled kind comes with every tree. Without the mod nothing changes. `dynamic_trees.enabled` in the worksites server config switches it off.
